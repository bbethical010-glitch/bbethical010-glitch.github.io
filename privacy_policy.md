1. INTRODUCTION
Welcome to Meme Capsule.
This Privacy Policy explains how Meme Capsule ("Meme Capsule", "we", "us", or "our"),
as the operator of memecapsule.wtf (the "Website"), collects, uses, and processes
personal data when you visit or interact with the Website.
This Privacy Policy applies only to the Website.
The Meme Capsule mobile application is a separate product and is not governed by this
Privacy Policy. The mobile application may have separate privacy practices and notices.
This Privacy Policy is focused on the Website's operations in India and is intended to
operate consistently with applicable Indian privacy and data-protection law, including
the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the Digital Personal
Data Protection Rules, 2025 ("DPDP Rules"), and other applicable Indian law, to the
extent applicable and in force from time to time.
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We seek to collect only information reasonably necessary for operating the Website,
responding to enquiries, and understanding Website usage.
2.1 Contact Form
When you use the Website's contact form, we collect:
• your name;
• your email address; and
• the message you choose to submit.
The message field allows you to provide information of your choosing.
Please do not voluntarily submit unnecessary sensitive or confidential information
through the contact form, including passwords, payment information, government
identification numbers, authentication credentials, or other information that is not
necessary for your enquiry.
2.2 Website Analytics
The Website collects technical and usage information that does not directly identify
you by name or contact details through Google Analytics 4 (GA4).
This may include:
• browser type;
• operating system;
• approximate, non-identifying location information;
• referring URL;
• pages viewed;
• page interactions;
• general Website usage information; and
• related technical analytics information.
Google Analytics is configured for website analytics and is not used by Meme Capsule
for advertising or remarketing.
3. HOW WE USE PERSONAL DATA
We use personal data for purposes connected with operating and improving the
Website, including:
• providing and maintaining the Website;
• understanding how visitors use the Website;
• improving Website content, functionality, and performance;
• responding to enquiries submitted through the contact form;
• communicating with individuals regarding their enquiries;
• understanding general Website traffic and usage;
• detecting or addressing misuse, abuse, or technical issues; and
• complying with applicable law or responding to lawful requests.
We do not use contact-form information merely because it has been provided to us for
unrelated purposes.
4. CONTACT FORM AND CONSENT
The Website's contact form collects only your name, email address, and message.
Before submitting the form, you will be presented with a consent checkbox linked to this
Privacy Policy.
The checkbox will state:
"I have read the Privacy Policy and consent to the processing of my personal data
for the purpose of responding to my enquiry."
The checkbox will be unchecked by default and will require an affirmative action
before the form can be submitted.
The consent is limited to receiving, reviewing, understanding, and responding to your
enquiry. It does not constitute consent to unrelated processing, advertising,
behavioural profiling, marketing communications, or future uses unrelated to your
enquiry.
Where applicable law provides a right to withdraw consent, you may contact Meme
Capsule at memecapsule.app@gmail.com.
Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before
withdrawal.
Meme Capsule will maintain an appropriate record of consent where required or
reasonably necessary to demonstrate that consent was obtained.
The contact-form consent mechanism does not replace any separate consent or
verification requirement that may apply to children's personal data.
5. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES
The Website uses cookies and similar technologies, including cookies associated
with Google Analytics.
Cookies may be used for:
• essential Website functionality;
• security and technical operation; and
• analytics and understanding Website usage.
Google Analytics cookies are used for analytics purposes and are not used by Meme
Capsule for advertising or remarketing.
Where a consent mechanism is required for non-essential cookies under applicable
law, Meme Capsule will obtain the required consent before activating those cookies
and will provide an appropriate means of declining or withdrawing such consent.
The Website's actual cookie configuration may change as its technical implementation
changes. This Privacy Policy will be updated where a material change affects the
purposes or manner of cookie-based processing.
6. GOOGLE ANALYTICS AND THIRD-PARTY PROCESSING
Meme Capsule uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) provided by Google to understand how
visitors use the Website.
Google Analytics is configured for website analytics and is not used by Meme Capsule
for advertising or remarketing.
Analytics information
The Website collects technical and usage information that does not directly identify
you by name or contact details through GA4, including browser type, operating
system, approximate non-identifying location, referring URL, and page interactions.
GA4 uses IP information at collection for purposes such as determining approximate
geographic information and routing data, but Google states that IP addresses are
discarded before the data is logged or stored by Google Analytics. Meme Capsule does
not implement a separate custom IP-address collection or tracking system through the
Website's GA4 implementation.
Analytics retention
The Website's GA4 user-level and event-level analytics data is configured to the
standard 2-month retention period.
Analytics data is subject to the retention configuration maintained in the relevant
Google Analytics property.
Processing outside India
Google Analytics is operated by Google and may process Website analytics information
using infrastructure located outside India.
Accordingly, information collected through Google Analytics may be processed outside
India in accordance with Google's applicable data-processing practices and applicable
law.
Meme Capsule does not independently determine the geographic location of Google's
infrastructure used to process the analytics information.
If the Website's Google Analytics configuration materially changes in a way that affects
the purposes or manner of processing described in this Privacy Policy, this Privacy
Policy may be updated accordingly.
7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
The Website is accessible to users of all ages. Meme Capsule does not impose an 18+
restriction on access to the Website.
We recognise that children may access the Website and may potentially use the
contact form or be exposed to Website analytics.
We do not intentionally request unnecessary personal data from children.
Where applicable Indian law requires specific safeguards for processing personal data
relating to children, Meme Capsule will implement those safeguards.
In particular, where verifiable parental or lawful-guardian consent is required, Meme
Capsule will use a separate verification mechanism rather than relying solely on a
child's completion of the ordinary contact-form consent checkbox.
Website-specific verification mechanism
Meme Capsule will implement a Website-specific verification mechanism designed
to satisfy the applicable verifiable parental-consent requirements under Indian
law, including the requirements applicable under the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules
when those provisions are in force.
The mechanism will be implemented using appropriate technical and organisational
measures and will maintain an appropriate audit record of the verification and consent
process.
The mechanism will be adapted as necessary to reflect the verification requirements
applicable under Indian law at the time it is used.
Meme Capsule will not knowingly process a child's personal data in a manner
prohibited by applicable Indian law.
If Meme Capsule becomes aware that a child's personal data has been processed
without a legally required parental or lawful-guardian consent, Meme Capsule will take
appropriate steps to stop the relevant processing and delete the affected personal
data where required by applicable law.
The Website's analytics configuration will also be reviewed and adjusted as necessary
to address requirements applicable to children's personal data.
8. SHARING AND DISCLOSURE
Meme Capsule does not indiscriminately disclose personal data.
Personal data may be disclosed where reasonably necessary:
• to operate the Website;
• to use Google Analytics;
• to respond to lawful legal or governmental requests;
• to comply with applicable law;
• to investigate suspected unlawful activity or misuse; or
• to protect the Website, its users, or the rights and safety of others.
For Website analytics, information is processed by Google through Google Analytics as
described in Section 6.
Meme Capsule does not sell personal data for monetary consideration.
Meme Capsule does not intentionally use contact-form information for unrelated
purposes or add individuals to promotional mailing lists solely because they submitted
an enquiry.
