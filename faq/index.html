Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Meme Capsule.

06 — QUESTIONS

Questions

Is Meme Capsule free?

+
Yes. The app is completely free to download and use. There are occasional ads but they are infrequent.

Where do the memes come from?

+
Our curated collection of thousands of memes in our capsule library, blended with fresh trending internet humor.

Can I control what memes I see?

+
No. That is the entire point. Pure randomness. No algorithm, no personalisation, no filter bubble.

How do I save a meme?

+
Tap the SAVE IT button below any meme and it goes straight to your phone gallery.

Is there inappropriate content?

+
We filter out NSFW content from all sources. The app is designed to be safe for general audiences.

How do I report a problem?

+
Use the contact form on this page or email us directly. We respond to every message.

Is my data collected?

+
We collect only anonymous usage analytics. No personal data is stored. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Why is the app called Meme Capsule?

+
Each meme is like a capsule — you don't know what's inside until you open it.

What Android version does Meme Capsule require?

+
Meme Capsule requires Android 8.0 (API level 26) or higher. It is compatible with the vast majority of Android devices currently in use.

How many memes are in the Meme Capsule collection?

+
The curated collection currently contains over 5,000 hand-picked meme images stored in our cloud library. The collection is regularly updated with new content.

Is Meme Capsule available in India?

+
Yes. Meme Capsule is available in India and all other countries where the Google Play Store operates. The app is free to download with no regional restrictions.

Does Meme Capsule work offline?

+
Meme Capsule requires an internet connection to fetch new memes from the collection. However, memes saved to your Meme Vault are stored locally on your device and can be viewed offline at any time.

Is there a Meme Capsule iOS or iPhone version?

+
Meme Capsule is currently available only for Android devices on the Google Play Store. An iOS version is not available at this time.

How do I share a meme from Meme Capsule to WhatsApp?

+
Tap the SHARE THIS button below any meme to open the Android native share sheet. Select WhatsApp from the list of available apps and the meme will be shared instantly without leaving the Meme Capsule app.

What is the Meme Vault?

+
The Meme Vault is your personal saved memes collection inside Meme Capsule. When you tap the VAULT button on any meme, it is saved to your vault and can be viewed, shared, or downloaded at any time. Your vault is stored locally on your device.

Why is there no algorithm in Meme Capsule?

+
Meme Capsule was designed specifically to avoid the filter bubble problem that affects most social media platforms. By delivering completely random memes with no personalisation, the app ensures that every session is unpredictable and every user sees a different variety of content.

How often is the meme collection updated?

+
The curated meme library is updated regularly as new content is added, alongside a continuous stream of fresh trending internet humor in real time.

Who built Meme Capsule?

+
Meme Capsule was designed and developed by Pratham Pandey, an independent developer based in India. For press enquiries or collaborations, contact memecapsule.app@gmail.com.
